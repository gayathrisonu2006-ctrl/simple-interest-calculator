# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a friendly and welcoming experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and polite.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Helping other contributors.
- Respecting different opinions.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including repositories, discussions, and other project activities.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers, who will review and address reports fairly.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take appropriate action depending on the seriousness of the violation.
